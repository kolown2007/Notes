**2050.** Earth is a world broken beyond repair. With global superpowers collapsed into dust, humanity has splintered into hyper-isolated strongholds: **Barangays**.

The downfall began in **2026**, when an AI mega-factory was constructed in Luzon to trigger the birth of a quantum AGI.

To power the core, scientists activated a dormant black hole inside the Moon.

The lunar anomaly went volatile, unleashing erratic, mountain-scaling tides that now sweep away whole civilizations without warning.

In a world dictated by deadly waters, prediction is total control. Only machine intelligence could calculate the next flood cycle, elevating local networks into ruthless **AI Despots**.

Driven by pure desperation, these rogue enclaves now slaughter each other for remaining resources in a brutal global tribal war: **InterBarangay**.

In a final attempt to rewrite history, a small resistance group from the **Visayas** dispatched a USB drive back toward 2026 containing the original AGI blueprint—hoping to destroy the threat before it ever wakes up.

Stretching across the temporal rift between 2050 and 2026 lie **888 Time Stations** hidden deep inside the wormhole.

**Your Mission:** Escort the USB payload and survive the journey to the next station inside the wormhole. Clear the corridor, pass the drive forward, and save the future.
