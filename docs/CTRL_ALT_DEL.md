**"HOME/PAGE** is an exhibition structured as a living paradox. Anchored in the mechanics of a causal loop, the exhibition collapses the boundaries between the physical street and the virtual interface. 

Through a studio practice where digital screenshots are meticulously translated into paintings before being re-digitized as virtual game assets, the artist mirrors the closed feedback loops of modern software protocols and algorithmic confinement. 

The recurring promise of an 'Exit' within the gallery becomes a structural impossibility—proving that in contemporary society, our frantic attempts to log off only serve to tighten the loop of our eventual digital re-absorption."
