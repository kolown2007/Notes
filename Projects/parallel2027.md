### Project Proposal: Parallel 2027

**Concept Overview** 
_Parallel 2027_ serves as a successor to _Parallel 2018_, presented during the Manila Biennale, where KoloWn intervened in the Intramuros landscape with fake historical markers crafted from wood and tarp. While the 2018 iteration interrogated physical space and public memory, _Parallel 2027_ shifts its focus to the museum archive as a site of speculative history.

KoloWn will engage with the **Vargas Museum’s diverse collection of non-art objects and personal memorabilia**, specifically those acquired or produced between the 1920s and the 1940s. By treating these items—ranging from stamps, coins, and official correspondence to personal ephemera—as "silent witnesses" to the era, the project investigates how objects store meaning and how we choose to interpret it.

Through a combination of photography, 3D scanning, and digital manipulation, these artifacts will be recontextualized to craft alternative, speculative narratives. The resulting work will be presented via interactive web-based platforms, designed to bridge the gap between digital engagement and immersive onsite projections within the museum.

The project excavates the complex histories bridging the Great Depression, the Tydings-McDuffie Act, the Commonwealth government, and World War II. By deconstructing non-art objects from this era, KoloWn proposes new ways of understanding their resonance in the present, drawing stark parallels between historical tensions and contemporary crises.
