

_Souvenir Surplus_ is a project speculating on artifacts from worlds that don't exist. Inspired by tourism memorabilia, it fabricates magnetic objects, faux ruins, and fictional relics from alternate timelines and spaces. Ultimately, the project treats these pieces as tangible memories of the never-have-been.  **"Walang naging tayo"**
